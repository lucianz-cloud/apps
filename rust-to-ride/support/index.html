Rust to Ride Support

Help for the garage

Need help? Email lucianz.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Tell us your phone or tablet model, the game version (shown on the game's App Store or Google Play page) and what happened.

Playing

How does it work?

Buy a wreck from a scrapyard, roll it into your garage and fix it part by part. Tap a glowing part on the car (or open All parts) to start its next job. When every part is done, finish the car, then sell it for coins or keep it in your showroom. Use the coins to buy the next wreck.

What are the two kinds of puzzle?

Screw jobs: tap screws to drop them into the case of their colour. A full case drives off; screws with nowhere to go wait in the tray. If the tray fills up, the job is jammed, so plan the order. Paint and fluid jobs: pour between cups until each cup holds one colour. Every puzzle can be solved.

What do the powers do?

Restart is always free. Revert takes back your last move and Hint shows a good next move; you can earn one of either by watching an ad. Auto makes three good moves for you and comes in the Mechanic Pack.

How do I open more scrapyards?

Restore cars. Harbour Salvage opens after your first restored car, Desert Boneyard after three and The Special Yard after six.

How do garage bays work?

Each bay holds one car you are working on. You start with 3 and can have up to 6. A new bay costs a small purchase or 5 ads; ads you watch towards a bay are remembered.

How do I change the language?

The game uses your phone's language when it has it (13 languages). To pick another, go to Settings → Language.

Is there help for colour-blind players or for calmer play?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Colour symbols for a shape on every paint and fluid, and Reduce motion for calmer effects. The game also follows your phone's reduce-motion setting. Sound and vibration can be switched off there too.

Ads and purchases

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play when you tap to watch one for a reward. There are no banner ads and no ads that start on their own.

An ad didn't give me the reward

The reward is given when the ad finishes. If you close it early or lose your connection, try again. If an ad isn't available right now, wait a minute and try again.

What can I buy?

The Mechanic Pack (10 mixed powers), extra garage bays and Fast buy (take a car without spending coins). Everything in the game can also be reached by playing.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close the game completely and open it again: it checks with the store when it starts. If it is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and your store receipt number (not your card details).

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.

Your progress

Where is my garage saved?

Only on your phone. There's no account. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include it, so a new phone restored from that backup gets your garage back.

How do I start over?

On the title screen, tap Start over. Your coins and cars go back to the start; anything you bought (bays, powers) stays. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The game froze or went back to the garage

If a screen fails, the game takes you back to the garage with a message and keeps your progress. If it keeps happening, email us your device model and what you were doing.
