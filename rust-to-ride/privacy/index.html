Privacy Policy

Rust to Ride · Effective 8 November 2026

In short: Rust to Ride has no accounts and no server. Your garage, cars, coins and settings stay on your phone. The only outside services are the optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob), which you choose to watch, and purchases through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email, card or location.

Who we are

Rust to Ride is made by Lucian Zamfir, an independent developer ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, email lucianz.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

To work, the game saves the following only on the phone, in the app's own storage:

  • your progress: coins, the cars in your garage and showroom, finished jobs and parts, and which scrapyards are open;
  • what you own: garage bays, powers (such as Revert, Hint and Auto) and how many ads you have watched towards a reward;
  • settings: sound, vibration, colour symbols, reduce motion and language;
  • which of Gus's tips you have already seen.

None of this is sent to us. We have no server and cannot see it.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup), if you have it turned on, may include this data so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under your account, and we cannot access it.

Rewarded ads

Ads only play when you tap a button to watch one in return for a reward (a Revert, a Hint, an extra tray slot or progress towards a garage bay). There are no banner ads and no ads that start on their own.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, AdMob's code in the app may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone and iPad, Advertising ID on Android), the device model and operating system, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with the ad. Google handles this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the app asks your permission before it can use the advertising identifier for tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, you still get ads and rewards, but they are not personalised. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad, and you can choose not to see personalised ads.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads on some phones).

We do not receive the advertising identifier or anything that identifies you. AdMob gives us only totals, such as how many ads were shown.

In-app purchases

The Mechanic Pack, garage bays and Fast buy are optional purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never see your card or account details: the app only asks the store, on the phone, whether a purchase went through.

Sharing a restored car

When a car is restored you can tap Share to send a picture of it. This opens your phone's own share sheet, and the picture goes only where you choose to send it. Nothing is shared without that tap.

Permissions

Rust to Ride uses the internet only to load ads and to talk to the App Store or Google Play. It does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Store statistics

Apple and Google show developers summary statistics in their dashboards (such as installs, crashes and countries). Those come from the stores under their own privacy policies, not from the game, and cannot identify you.

Children

Rust to Ride is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not made specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example in a support email), write to us and we will delete it.

Deleting data

In the game, Start over on the title screen resets your coins and cars (anything you bought stays). Deleting the app removes everything it kept from the phone. We hold no game data about you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete. For data held by Google for ads, see My Ad Center.

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to personalised ads. For anything we may hold (such as a support email), write to lucianz.app@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. A change that collects more data would come with an app update and would be described here first.
