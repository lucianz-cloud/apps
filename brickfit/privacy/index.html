Privacy Policy

Brickfit: Family Block Puzzle · Effective 8 November 2026

In short: Brickfit collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. Everything the game keeps stays on the phone, and nothing about you or your children leaves it.

Who we are

Brickfit is made by Lucian Zamfir, an independent developer ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, email lucianz.app@gmail.com.

What we never collect

The app never sends anyone's name, age, photo, voice, contacts or location to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no chat and no ads, and it does not use device or advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or AAID). It does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

What stays on your device

To work, Brickfit saves the following only on the phone, in the app's own storage:

  • players' first names or nicknames, and the avatars they made;
  • scores, stars, saved games and daily puzzle results;
  • minutes played each day, for the grown-ups area and the play-time limits;
  • settings, such as sound, language and play-time rules;
  • whether the Family Pass is owned.

None of this is sent anywhere. Brickfit has no server and we cannot see it. The game only asks for first names or nicknames.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup), if you have it turned on, may include this data so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under your account, and we cannot access it.

The Family Pass

The Family Pass is an optional one-time purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, behind a check that a grown-up is buying. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never see card or account details: the app only asks the store, on the phone, whether the pass is owned.

Ratings

The rating prompt only appears from the grown-ups area, behind the parent check, or right after a grown-up buys the pass. Rating the app is handled by Apple or Google.

Store statistics

Apple and Google show developers summary statistics in their dashboards (such as installs, crashes and countries). Those come from the stores under their own privacy policies, not from the app, and cannot identify you.

Children's privacy

Brickfit is made for children and families. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children. It is designed to follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, the Apple Kids Category guidelines and the Google Play Families policy.

Deleting data

In the app, Settings → Reset stars and scores (hold for 3 seconds) clears stars and scores, and Who's playing? → Edit removes a player. Deleting the app removes everything it kept from the phone. The Family Pass can be restored from the store with Restore purchase. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete.

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Please leave out your child's name and other personal details about your child.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. For anything we may hold (such as a support email), write to lucianz.app@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. A change that collects any data would need an app update you can see, and would be described here first.
