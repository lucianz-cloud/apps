Brickfit Support

Help for grown-ups

Need help? Email lucianz.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Tell us your phone model and what happened. Please leave out your child's name.

Playing

How does it work?

Drag bricks from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Every line cleared adds a star to the family star jar, and an empty board is worth five. The game ends when none of the bricks in the tray fit. There are no timers.

What is the daily puzzle?

One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Finish it to keep your streak going and compare scores with the family.

How do players work?

Up to 5 players can share one phone. Each has an avatar, a saved game and a best score. Tap Who's playing? to switch, add or edit a player. Stars from every player go into one family jar.

How do I unlock themes and brick styles?

With stars from the family jar. Every theme and style can be earned by playing, except Space, which comes with the Family Pass.

How do I change the language?

Brickfit uses your phone's language when it has it. To pick another one, go to Settings → Language.

Is there help for colour-blind players or for calmer play?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Colour-blind bricks for patterns on every brick, and Calm mode for softer effects. The game also follows your phone's reduce-motion setting.

For grown-ups

How do I open the grown-ups area?

Go to Settings → Grown-ups area and answer the times-table question. Play time, progress and purchases are all behind it.

How do I set a play-time limit?

In the grown-ups area, open Play time and choose Off, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes a day for each player. A note appears 5 minutes before the end, and play stops once the move in hand is done. On the "That's all for today" screen a grown-up can add 15 minutes for today. With the Family Pass you can also set custom minutes, school days and weekends, bedtime and break reminders.

The Family Pass

What is it?

An optional one-time purchase for the whole family on the phone: every theme and style right away plus the Space theme, avatar hats, glasses and pets, extra sticker sets, replays of past daily puzzles, party effects, more play-time rules, and a week and a month of progress. There is no subscription.

How do I restore the pass on a new phone?

Open the Family Pass screen from the grown-ups area and tap Already bought it? Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The app also checks with the store every time it starts, so a pass bought on another device turns on by itself.

Can the rest of my family use it?

On iPhone and iPad, the pass works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your Apple family gets it. On Android, it works for the Google account that bought it.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.

Your data

Where is progress saved?

Only on the phone. There's no account. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include it, so a new phone restored from that backup gets the players, stars and scores back.

How do I start over?

Go to Settings → Reset stars and scores and hold the button for 3 seconds. To remove one player, use Who's playing? → Edit. See the Privacy Policy for details.
