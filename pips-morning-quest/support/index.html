Pip's Morning Quest Support

Help for grown-ups

Need help? Email lucianz.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Tell us your phone model and what happened. Please leave out your child's name.

Getting started

How do I open the grown-up area?

Press and hold the Grown-ups button for 3 seconds. It locks again after 5 minutes without touches, or when you leave the app.

How do I change my child's morning steps or start time?

Open the grown-up area, choose the child, then edit the routine. Reminders update automatically.

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Pip's Morning Quest in your phone's Settings. On Android, also check that battery saving isn't blocking the app. Opening the app once will reschedule all reminders.

The read-aloud voice sounds odd

The app uses your phone's built-in voice. You can download a better voice in your phone's accessibility or language settings, or have a grown-up read the story instead.

Family plan

What does the Family plan add?

The first child profile is free forever. The Family plan lets you add a second and third child.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open the grown-up area, go to the plan screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not in the app:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

What happens if the plan ends?

Extra child profiles are paused, never deleted. Their progress comes back if you subscribe again. The first child keeps working as usual.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.

Your data

Where is my data saved?

Only on your device. There's no account and we never see it. See the Privacy Policy.

Will I lose everything if I delete the app or change phones?

Deleting the app deletes its data. There is no cloud sync. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data and can restore it to a new phone.

How do I erase everything?

Grown-up area → More → Delete all data.
