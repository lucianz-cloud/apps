Privacy Policy

Pip's Morning Quest · Effective 31 October 2026

In short: Pip's Morning Quest has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter is saved only on your device. We never receive it.

Who we are

Pip's Morning Quest is made by Lucian Zamfir, an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have questions about privacy, email lucianz.app@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • each child's first name, age, chosen animal buddy and optional buddy name;
  • morning routine steps, start times and reminder settings;
  • progress, such as completed steps, stars, story chapters and story choices;
  • an optional checklist for a grown-up;
  • app settings and whether a Family plan is active.

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. Stories are personalised on the device itself. We have no server that stores your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, audio or usage data. It contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications that the device schedules for itself. No push server is used and no notification token leaves the device.

Read-aloud voice

When the app reads a step or a story out loud, it uses the text-to-speech voice built into your phone's operating system. We do not receive any of that text.

Purchases

The optional Family plan is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether a Family plan is active on your account.

Children's privacy

Pip's Morning Quest is designed for children aged 5–8 and their grown-ups. Because no personal information leaves the device, we do not collect personal information from children. This is in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families policies.

Grown-up areas, purchases and any link that leaves the app are protected by a parental gate.

Backups

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup), if you have it turned on, may include this app's data. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under your account. We cannot access it.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the grown-up area and choose More → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes all its data from the device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you.

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Please do not include a child's name or other personal details about a child in your message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Since the app's data lives only on your device, you control it directly. For anything we may hold (such as a support email), write to lucianz.app@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects what data the app handles, we will also say so in the app's release notes.
