Animal Phone Support

Help for grown-ups

Need help? Email lucianz.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Tell us your phone model and what happened. Please leave out your child's name.

Playing

How does it work?

Your child dials a 3-digit number to video-call a cartoon animal. Each new friend gets a card in the Agenda. The first digit is the habitat: 0 Ocean, 1 Farm, 2 Forest, 3 Desert, 4 Savanna, 5 Taiga, 6 Jungle, 7 Arctic, 8 Space, 9 Mythical. Find all the main friends of a habitat to unlock its theme.

Are there secret numbers?

Yes. Some holiday friends have hidden 4-digit numbers. Try important dates!

Does the app listen to or film my child?

No. The app does not use the microphone or camera. The "speak" button and the child's picture in a call are only cartoons.

How do I open the grown-up area?

Tap the grown-ups button and answer the question on the Parent Gate. Purchases, videos, ratings and links are all behind it.

How do I change the language?

The app uses your phone's language. To change it, go to For grown-ups → App language.

Friend reminders don't arrive

Turn on Friend reminders in the grown-up area, and check that notifications are allowed for Animal Phone in your phone's Settings. A reminder only comes after 2 days without play.

Purchases and videos

What can I buy?

  • Unlock all themes & remove ads. This doesn't unlock friends: they are still found by calling.
  • Group calls. Invite a second animal friend into a call.
  • Everything bundle. Both of the above together, for less.

All are one-off purchases. There is no subscription.

What are the videos?

Short kid-safe, non-personalised videos that a grown-up can choose to watch in the grown-up area to unlock a theme or get a Magic hint. They never play during calls or on your child's screens.

How do I restore my purchases on a new phone?

Go to For grown-ups → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.

Your data

Where is progress saved?

Only on your device. There's no account. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include it, so a new phone restored from that backup gets the friends and themes back.

How do I start over?

Go to For grown-ups → Reset progress. This erases friends found, call history and unlocked themes. Purchases and settings stay.

What are "anonymous usage counts"?

Daily totals, such as how many calls were made, with no name, no ID and nothing about your child. You can see them and turn them off in For grown-ups → Share anonymous usage counts. See the Privacy Policy for details.
