Privacy Policy

Animal Phone · Effective 31 October 2026

In short: Animal Phone collects no personal data about your child. There is no account and no login. Progress stays on your device. The app can send anonymous daily usage counts, which you can turn off. Optional videos for grown-ups are never personalised.

Who we are

Animal Phone is made by Lucian Zamfir, an independent developer ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, email lucianz.app@gmail.com.

What we never collect

The app never collects, records or stores your child's name, age, birthday, photo, voice, contacts or location. It has no accounts and does not use device or advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or AAID). It does not use the microphone or the camera. The "speak" button and the child's picture during a call are only cartoons.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following only on your device:

  • friends found, call history and unlocked themes;
  • settings such as app language, sounds and reminders;
  • which purchases are unlocked.

We do not receive this information. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup), if you have it turned on, may include it so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under your account, and we cannot access it.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn what to improve, the app can send anonymous usage counts once a day, for example which buttons are tapped, how many numbers were dialled and how long calls last (in rough bands). Each daily packet holds only the counts plus the app version, app language and operating-system version.

  • It contains no name, no user, device or advertising ID, no location and no free text.
  • Nothing links one day's counts to another day's, and there are no exact times, only the day.
  • Our server discards the IP address.

You can see what would be sent today and turn this off at any time in For grown-ups → Share anonymous usage counts. When it is off, the app counts and sends nothing, and it works exactly the same.

Apple and Google also show us summary statistics in their developer dashboards (such as installs, crashes and countries). Those come from the stores under their own privacy policies, not from the app.

Optional videos

The app has no ads that a child can see. Grown-ups can choose to watch short videos in the grown-up area, behind the Parent Gate, to unlock a theme or get a hint. They never play during calls or on the child's screens.

These videos come from an ad network certified for children's apps. They are contextual and not personalised, and no advertising ID is used. To deliver a video, the ad network may receive basic technical information such as the device type, operating system and IP address, which it handles under its own privacy policy. Buying "Unlock all themes & remove ads" removes the videos.

Purchases

Purchases are one-off and are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play behind the Parent Gate. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, and we never see your payment details. The app only asks the store which purchases you own.

Friend reminders

If you turn them on, reminders are local notifications that the device schedules for itself. No push server is used and no notification token leaves the device.

Ratings and links

The rating prompt, this policy and any other link that leaves the app sit behind the Parent Gate. Rating the app is handled by Apple or Google.

Children's privacy

Animal Phone is made for children aged 3 and up. It is designed to follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, the Apple Kids Category guidelines and the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting data

In For grown-ups → Reset progress you can erase friends found, call history and unlocked themes. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device. Purchases can be restored from the store with Restore purchases. Anonymous counts we have received cannot be linked to you, so there is nothing about you to look up or delete.

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Please leave out your child's name and other personal details about your child.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. For anything we may hold (such as a support email), write to lucianz.app@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects what data the app handles, we will also say so in the app's release notes.
